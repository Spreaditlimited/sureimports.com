# Free catalog China sourcing SEO

## Search intent

The free catalog pages serve buyers who want to source or import a named product from China, then request a checked supplier quote for delivery to Nigeria. The PDF's RMB prices are supplier references, not final Nigerian offers. The Futura wig PDF uses USD reference prices.

Each landing page uses a product-specific “source” or “import ... from China” title and H1, explains what is inside its PDF, gives checks for that product category, and asks for enough detail to prepare a useful quote. The pages link to the broader China-to-Nigeria import guide. No page claims confirmed stock or a guaranteed delivered price.

## Page mapping examples

- Futura wigs: “Import Futura wigs from China”; supported by “import wigs from china.” The page distinguishes Futura fibre, JY style code, length, colour and lace options.
- Phone cases: “Source phone cases from China”; supported by “wholesale phone cases china” and “phone cases in bulk from china.” The page asks for model mix, material, finish and quantity per variant.
- Charging cables: “Import charging cables from China”; an intent-specific extension of the measured phone accessories cluster. The page asks for both connectors, power rating and data capability.
- Car chargers: “Import car chargers from China”; a relevant exact product query appeared without a reported volume. The page asks for port layout and output rather than implying every charger has the same specification.

The individual catalog page, rather than the supplier reports index, is the intended organic landing page for each product category.
