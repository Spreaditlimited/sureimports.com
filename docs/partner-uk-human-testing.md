# UK partner human acceptance checklist

Use a dedicated test business. Real charges and transfers require explicit approval; never edit production payment statuses to simulate success.

1. Admin → Partners → Countries: review GBP fee and trial days. Zero means free; first activation freezes these terms.
2. Apply as a UK individual/company and submit identity, address and customer-acquisition evidence. Confirm submitted fields are locked.
3. Approve business fit and verification in Admin. Missing address, identity/video or own-name payout confirmation must keep agreement acceptance blocked.
4. Partner → Verification: read and accept the agreement. Activation, Storefront and Billing should become available without another admin action. Check the branded email.
5. Customize the storefront, connect the domain through ownership/DNS/HTTPS/routing checks, then publish.
6. As a separate storefront customer, create a UK-bound order. Review GBP totals and complete only an approved payment. Refresh must not duplicate receipts.
7. As partner, approve the paid order. Sure Imports should receive the operational order and the partner's receiving address.
8. Test approved shipping adjustment and partial refund flows. Check original-currency amounts, notices and proportionate product-earnings adjustments. Shipping must not earn commission.
9. Operational delivery to the partner must leave earnings pending. Customer receipt/collection confirmation releases eligible earnings.
10. Save an own-name PayPal destination using the partner account's email code. Request an approved small withdrawal. Admin submits it; only canonical individual payout success marks it paid.
11. After a paid plan's trial ends, approve the displayed monthly subscription. Refresh for the receipt and paid period. Cancel and confirm already-paid access remains.
12. Expired unpaid access must block new checkout/publication, not existing fulfilment, refunds or available earnings withdrawals.

Live monthly renewal/reversal behaviour requires provider events or an approved provider test environment. Rollback-only automated suites cover these branches without moving funds.
