# Product Backlog

## Paid Import Manufacturer Directory

Later phase after the current lead-magnet automation is complete.

Concept: a premium, paid PDF product for China importers.

Working direction:
- A serious research document covering top manufacturers in China across 100 niches.
- Each niche should include 10 vetted manufacturers where possible.
- Include company websites, phone numbers, WhatsApp/contact channels where publicly available and verifiable.
- The document must be carefully researched, well written, and positioned as a paid product.
- This product should be marketed inside the 52-week import education email sequence.

Important standards:
- Do not invent manufacturer details, contacts, certifications, or capacity claims.
- Verify sources before publishing.
- Separate “verified direct manufacturer” from “trading company/distributor” where evidence supports the distinction.
