# 52-Week Import Education Email Sequence

Cadence:
- Welcome email immediately after subscription with the lead magnet download.
- Email 1 starts one week later.
- One educational email per week.
- Every email should teach one solid importing concept and include a soft CTA for the paid manufacturer-directory product once available.

Rules:
- Do not invent live exchange rates, customs rates, shipping rates, supplier claims, or regulatory requirements.
- Use practical examples, but label estimates clearly.
- Match CTA to subscriber context where possible: procurement, supplier verification, shipping, supplier payment, phones, laptops, or corporate sourcing.
- Keep the product CTA short and relevant. The education must stand on its own.
