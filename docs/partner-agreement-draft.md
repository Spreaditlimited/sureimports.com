# Sure Imports Partner Services Agreement — working draft

Status: DRAFT FOR BUSINESS AND NIGERIAN LEGAL REVIEW. Not published terms, not an executed agreement, and not approved for customer or partner acceptance.

This document records proposed agreement content and the requirements for replacing the admin's manual “Partner agreement reference” with verifiable electronic acceptance. It does not change collection settings, activate businesses, or establish an escrow arrangement.

## 1. Parties and authority

Identify Sure Importers Limited (confirm its exact registered particulars before publication) and the partner's verified Nigerian business name, CAC number, registered address and notice email. Record the accepting representative's full name, role and authority to bind the business. For a registered business name, counsel must identify the proprietor(s) who are the contracting party, rather than treating the business name as a separate incorporated company.

The arrangement is an independent commercial services relationship. It does not create an employment relationship, equity partnership or authority to make commitments on the other party's behalf, except for any expressly agreed payment-collection authority.

## 2. What Sure Imports provides

Sure Imports provides the agreed branded storefront and partner workspace, supported API access, procurement from supported Chinese websites, order tracking and shipping to the partner's agreed delivery or collection point. Hosting, included support, inspection scope, optional services and exclusions must be described accurately in the service schedule.

The partner supplies product links and customer-approved specifications through the ordering system. Photo-only sourcing is not included in this service.

## 3. What the partner provides

The partner acquires customers, operates its business, checks customer orders before approval, communicates prices and realistic delivery expectations, handles customer support and complaints, and arranges onward delivery to its customers. It maintains accurate business, ownership, banking and contact information and complies with ongoing verification requirements.

Sure Imports fulfils to the partner, not individual partner customers. Direct customer communications should be limited to disclosed transactional, security, payment or legally required communications; no promise should prevent mandatory dispute or compliance handling.

No guaranteed sales, income, exclusive territory or delivery timeframe is offered unless expressly agreed in writing.

## 4. Ordering and approval

Describe the actual customer-created order lifecycle: customer submits links, variants and quantities; the partner reviews the request; the applicable quote and payment requirements are met; Sure Imports processes the approved, paid order.

Identify precisely which system status authorises a supplier purchase. Approval does not permit silent substitutions or extra charges. Price, exchange-rate, stock or specification changes requiring additional payment must be accepted before that additional commitment is made. Quotes must state validity and what is included.

An order's commercial terms are snapshotted when the applicable quote is accepted. Later configuration changes must not silently change existing accepted orders.

## 5. Commercial schedule and earnings

The signed commercial schedule must show:

- The applicable product service-charge rate, currently intended to be 15% of product cost.
- The partner's allocated rate, expressed explicitly as a percentage of product cost, not as a percentage of the service charge.
- Sure Imports' remaining service-charge share.
- The definition of product cost, currency conversion method, discounts and exclusions.
- Any hosting/platform fee, billing cycle, included services, applicable taxes and cancellation rules. No hosting fee amount has yet been approved.
- Earning eligibility, adjustments for partial purchases, refunds and reversals, and the settlement policy once agreed.

Example only: on a NGN 100,000 product cost and 15% service charge, the service charge is NGN 15,000. If the partner allocation is 5 percentage points of product cost, the partner earns NGN 5,000 and Sure Imports retains NGN 10,000. If the allocation is 10 points, those amounts reverse. Neither example selects the final rate for a particular partner.

Partners receive no shipping commission under this programme. Product purchase funds and shipping charges are not partner earnings. The separate affiliate programme and its commission rules must not be incorporated accidentally.

## 6. Payment collection and checkout disclosure

The intended model is NGN collection through the authorised Paystack integration, with applicable processing fees disclosed to and borne by the customer, subject to processor requirements and applicable law. A supported bank payment method inside the authorised checkout is distinct from an unrecorded direct transfer outside it.

Explain the collection relationship, the name the payer will see, refund channels and the partner's service-charge allocation. Verify with Paystack and Nigerian counsel whether the proposed partner-as-merchant-of-record arrangement is supported while Sure Imports owns the collecting integration. Do not imply that a subaccount alone establishes that legal status or changes checkout branding.

## 7. Settlements, holds and reserves — decision required

The published schedule must define the earning-release trigger, payout timing, provider processing delays, the evidence needed to establish delivery, and treatment of disputed orders. Distinguish Sure Imports delivery to the partner from the partner's delivery to its customer.

A split payment is not evidence of an escrow or discretionary hold/release facility. Do not promise a hold until delivery unless the provider arrangement actually supports it.

If reserves are introduced, specify the affected funds, reasons, calculation or cap, notification, review frequency, release conditions and challenge process. No reserve percentage, indefinite hold, release deadline or automatic debit authority is approved by this draft.

## 8. Refunds, chargebacks and negative balances

Explain cancellation before purchase, cancellation after a supplier commitment, supplier refunds, unavailable items, partial refunds, payment disputes and resulting earning reversals. Do not promise that all costs are refundable or that nothing is refundable irrespective of circumstances or law.

Allocate responsibility based on the cause and evidence: partner misinformation or unauthorised promises, supplier failure, procurement errors, transit loss or payment fraud must not be treated as identical. Set an evidence-submission and escalation process compatible with processor deadlines.

The final agreement must expressly settle liability, permissible offsets and recovery of any negative balance. A private allocation between Sure Imports and the partner does not remove obligations imposed on the Paystack account holder by Paystack's own agreement.

## 9. Products, inspection and shipping

Define prohibited goods, restricted products, authenticity/IP requirements, required import documentation and refusal rights. State who is responsible for information supplied about the goods and who arranges relevant regulatory approvals.

Describe inspection actually included, optional verification, shipment measurement and billing, insurance options, customs treatment, consolidation and storage charges. Explain estimates and delays without excluding responsibility for Sure Imports' own obligations.

Specify custody, proof of delivery/collection, risk transfer, damage or shortage evidence and claims handling. The exact risk-transfer point, claim windows and uninsured-loss treatment need explicit approval; do not invent them in the interface.

## 10. Customer relationship, branding and data

Recognise the partner's customer relationship. Recommend a clear prohibition on using partner customer data for unrelated Sure Imports marketing without an appropriate basis and permission. Do not claim ownership of individuals' personal data or promise perpetual exclusivity over customers independently approaching other businesses.

Set permissions for partner logos/content, the licensed use of the platform, payment disclosures, acceptable marketing claims and API credentials. The partner may not misrepresent itself as Sure Imports or promise unapproved prices or outcomes.

Include a data-protection schedule addressing purposes, lawful grounds, each party's actual controller/processor role for each activity, confidentiality, access controls, approved service providers, cross-border processing, incident cooperation, data-subject requests, retention and deletion. Do not use blanket consent as a substitute for all data-protection obligations.

## 11. Domains, hosting and platform access

Record domain ownership, authorised registration/connection, renewal responsibility, charges, DNS and HTTPS requirements, support boundaries and acceptable use. Distinguish a partner-owned domain from Sure Imports' software and templates.

Explain what happens to the domain, customer/order exports and storefront at termination. Do not promise a source-code transfer or uninterrupted service unless those are actually included. Domain transfer must remain subject to registrar rules and verified ownership, not arbitrary forfeiture.

## 12. Suspension, termination and open orders

Set reasonable suspension grounds including fraud, prohibited goods, security threats, material breaches and failed verification. Describe notice and opportunity to remedy where appropriate, urgent protective action, and an appeal/contact route.

Termination must address paid orders still in progress, goods in custody, refunds and disputes, unpaid contractual charges, earned amounts, permitted reserves, data retention/export and domain transition. Ending access must not erase payment records or automatically forfeit legitimate earnings.

## 13. Liability, complaints and legal terms

Include proportionate liability provisions, carefully scoped indemnities, confidentiality, force majeure, notices, assignment, severability, agreement priority and amendment procedures. Nigerian counsel must approve liability limits and any exclusions, statutory-rights treatment, governing law and the dispute-resolution forum.

Separate partner business obligations from consumer-facing storefront terms. The partner agreement alone does not provide the required customer order, privacy, delivery and refund disclosures.

## 14. Versions and acceptance

The accepted package comprises a named agreement version, the business-specific commercial schedule, applicable service/data schedules and their hashes. Provide a readable copy and a downloadable record. Material changes require appropriate notice and renewed acceptance; never overwrite the agreement previously accepted for an order.

## Electronic acceptance implementation requirements

Recommended sequence: business-fit review and verification -> approved commercial offer -> authorised representative accepts -> admin final activation. Acceptance alone must not approve KYC, activate a business, publish a storefront or enable collection.

1. Store immutable agreement versions with draft/published/retired status. Drafts cannot be accepted. Published content, referenced schedules and hashes cannot be edited in place.
2. Issue a business-specific offer with verified legal identity, exact commercial values and a revision. Changes invalidate the outstanding offer rather than rewriting an accepted record.
3. Show the full agreement and schedule in the existing partner dashboard design. Provide download, clear outstanding requirements and an unchecked, explicit acceptance confirmation identifying the representative's authority.
4. Authenticate the accepting business owner or an explicitly authorised signatory. Verify business ownership and current offer/version on the server. Do not trust a client-supplied rate, identity, text hash or acceptance timestamp.
5. Record a server-generated acceptance reference, partner and signer identifiers, signer's name and role, accepted offer/version/hash and server timestamp. Any extra technical evidence must have a defined purpose and retention policy; do not collect it indiscriminately.
6. Make acceptance atomic and idempotent. Reject retired/stale offers and ensure concurrent offer updates cannot produce acceptance of mismatched terms. Preserve historic records.
7. Replace the admin's editable reference with the verified acceptance record and document link. Final activation must validate the current accepted offer under the existing transaction locks alongside KYC, business fit and membership protections.
8. Queue a branded acceptance receipt and expose it in both dashboards. Email failure must not erase acceptance; existing retry processing should deliver it subsequently.
9. Test unauthorised acceptance, stale offers, changed rates/identity, double clicks, concurrent activation, downloads, mobile layout and email retries. Never create acceptance on behalf of an existing real business during migration.
