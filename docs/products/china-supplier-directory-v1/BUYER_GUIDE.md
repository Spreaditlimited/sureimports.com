# Buyer Guide Draft

## How to Use This Directory

Use this directory as a sourcing starting point, not as permission to pay any supplier immediately.

Every supplier lead should go through a verification process before money changes hands. The purpose of the directory is to help Nigerian importers move from "I do not know where to start" to "I have a shortlist to investigate properly."

## Before You Contact a Supplier

Prepare:

- product name and use case,
- target quantity,
- expected quality level,
- target landed cost range,
- packaging requirements,
- destination country and city,
- whether you need samples,
- whether you need branding or customization.

## Questions to Ask Suppliers

1. Are you the manufacturer, brand owner, distributor, or trading company?
2. Can you share your business license and export documents?
3. What is the MOQ for this product?
4. Can I order samples before bulk production?
5. What certifications or test reports are available for this product?
6. What is included in the quote?
7. What payment recipient name will appear on the invoice?
8. Can the goods be inspected before shipment?
9. Can you ship to the Sure Imports China warehouse or my appointed forwarder?
10. What packaging options are available?

## Supplier Scorecard

Score each supplier from 1-5:

- website and company identity clarity,
- product fit,
- response quality,
- sample availability,
- quote clarity,
- payment recipient consistency,
- inspection readiness,
- packaging/customization ability,
- export experience,
- risk concerns.

Do not choose based on lowest price alone.
