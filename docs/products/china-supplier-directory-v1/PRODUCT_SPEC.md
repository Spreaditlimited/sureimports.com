# China Supplier Directory for Nigerian Importers - V1 Product Spec

## Working Title

China Supplier Directory for Nigerian Importers: 25 Profitable Niches

## Positioning

This is not just a list of random supplier contacts. It is a practical sourcing starting point for Nigerian importers who do not know where to begin, what product categories to explore, or how to approach suppliers safely.

The product should help a buyer:

- understand 25 viable import niches,
- see examples of supplier/manufacturer leads,
- know what to verify before paying,
- avoid treating supplier contact details as proof of trust,
- compare suppliers using a repeatable scorecard,
- decide when to use Sure Imports Corporate Sourcing, LineScout, Ship With Us, or Buy From Chinese Websites.

## Version 1 Scope

- 25 niches.
- Minimum of 3 supplier leads per niche.
- Every published niche must have at least 3 suppliers with `official_site_contact_confirmed` status.
- If a niche cannot support at least 3 solid suppliers with official contact evidence, replace the niche.
- Extra supplier leads may be included only when they improve buyer usefulness and pass verification.
- Supplier details only from official/public sources.
- No invented phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, names, certifications, MOQ, prices, factory claims, or addresses.
- Every supplier entry must include source notes.

## Supplier Entry Fields

Required:

- niche
- supplier_name
- product_fit
- official_website
- source_type
- verified_from
- buyer_notes
- verification_status

Optional only when publicly listed by the supplier:

- email
- phone
- whatsapp
- address
- country_region
- official_contact_page
- relevant_product_page
- certifications_claimed

## Verification Levels

`official_site_confirmed`

The supplier/brand has an official website that confirms relevant product categories and/or official contact/support channels.

`official_site_contact_confirmed`

The supplier/brand has an official website and an official contact page or publicly listed support/contact details.

For final paid-product inclusion, a supplier only counts toward the 3-per-niche minimum when it has an official company-controlled contact route. Strongest evidence is a published email, phone number, WhatsApp number, contact form, or office address on the supplier's own website.

`marketplace_only`

Supplier appears only through a marketplace listing. This is not enough for the paid product unless there is a strong reason and a clear warning.

`needs_manual_verification`

Potentially useful lead, but not enough public evidence yet.

## Editorial Rules

- Use plain English for Nigerian business buyers.
- Do not present any supplier as "safe" or "verified" merely because they have a website.
- Prefer "lead", "starting point", or "company to investigate" over "recommended supplier".
- Mention that all buyers should still verify business registration, product samples, invoice terms, payment recipient, production capacity, and inspection options.
- Phone/WhatsApp fields must be blank unless the exact contact is published on an official company-controlled source.
- Do not publish any niche with fewer than 3 `official_site_contact_confirmed` suppliers.
- Remove or replace entries marked `needs_manual_verification` before the paid PDF is generated.

## Product Warnings

The PDF must include these warnings prominently:

- Supplier details can change.
- A public website does not prove legitimacy.
- Always verify the supplier before paying.
- Do not pay personal accounts unless independently verified.
- Ask for product samples, production photos/videos, and business documents where appropriate.
- For machines and technical equipment, use LineScout or another serious technical verification process.
