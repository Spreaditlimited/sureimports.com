# Google Merchant Center: Nigeria gadget shop

The shop sells gadgets sourced from China after payment, with estimated delivery to a customer's Nigerian address in 10–15 business days: 5–10 handling days and 5 transit days. Nationwide doorstep delivery is included in the displayed NGN price.

**Do not submit the current catalogue to Merchant Center yet.** Supplier stock is checked only after the customer orders. The site's `available` flag means an item can be ordered, not that its exact variant is confirmed available. Google requires feed availability to match the landing and checkout pages. `in_stock` requires confidence in timely fulfilment; `preorder` applies to unreleased goods; `backorder` requires an availability date for a temporarily unavailable item. None accurately represents supplier confirmation after payment. The site deliberately does not claim that orderable gadgets are in stock, and there is no live Merchant Center feed or account connection.

The owner confirmed that suppliers do **not** reserve exact variants and quantities before payment. A one-time supplier check therefore cannot itself guarantee stock. To make the catalogue reliably sourced, arrange one of these operational controls for each listed variant: stock owned or reserved by Sure Imports, a supplier commitment to fulfil a defined quantity for a defined period, or a dependable alternative supplier that can fulfil the same specification and delivery promise. Record the supplier, exact configuration, confirmed quantity, price, confirmation time and expiry. Recheck before payment, subtract paid quantities, and remove expired or depleted products from any feed. If no such control is possible, keep the product outside Merchant Center and state clearly that it is sourced after order.

Once that control is operating, generate a Nigeria-only feed with the exact checkout price, condition, image, verified product identifier, free nationwide shipping, and 5–10 handling plus 5 transit business days. Do not invent GTINs or manufacturer part numbers from internal SKUs. Audit fulfilment failures and suspend a product immediately when its promise cannot be met.

The owner must create a Google Merchant Center account, enter `https://www.sureimports.com` as the online store, verify and claim it using the existing Search Console owner or Google's offered verification method, and select Nigeria as the sole target country and NGN as currency. Once a truthful feed exists, add its URL under Settings → Data sources → Add product source and schedule a daily fetch. Under Shipping and returns, enter a Nigeria-only free doorstep policy with 5–10 handling and 5 transit days. Review the first fetch, product diagnostics, policy review and country availability. No Google Ads account or paid campaign is required for this preparatory work.
