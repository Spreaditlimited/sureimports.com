Buying salon equipment is not one decision. A styling chair, reclining barber chair and shampoo wash unit may all provide client seating, but their mechanisms, space requirements, installation needs and failure points differ. The right buying process starts with the service performed at each station, then checks dimensions, technical evidence, maintenance support and total landed cost.

This guide focuses on commercial salon and barbershop equipment in Nigeria. The Nigerian prices below are individual seller listings checked on 9 October 2026. They are examples, not a market price range, valuation or endorsement. Confirm current stock, variant, included parts, delivery and installation directly with the seller before paying.

Understand the main salon chair categories

Equipment Primary job Important buying checks
Styling chair Hair cutting, braiding, finishing and general styling Hydraulic travel, rotation lock, seat width, base stability, upholstery and working height
Reclining barber chair Haircuts, beard work, shaving and services requiring a tilted client position Recline mechanism, headrest, footrest movement, locked stability, load rating and rear clearance
Wash unit or shampoo station Washing and conditioning hair Basin material, neck support, chair position, mixer and hose contents, water connections, drainage and professional installation
Dryer or processor chair Seating beneath hood dryers or treatment processors Chair height, device clearance, power requirements and heat-resistant surrounding finishes
Pedicure or multi-function chair Services requiring leg support, powered movement or integrated water features Motor specifications, controls, water system, rated load, electrical protection and service parts

A beauty salon chair intended for styling should not be accepted as a barber chair merely because the supplier adds a loose footrest. A proper barber configuration may need a controlled reclining back, adjustable headrest and a leg or foot support that remains stable while the client changes position. Likewise, a wash unit is plumbing equipment as well as furniture.

How to evaluate a beauty salon chair technically

Hydraulic travel and working height

Ask for minimum and maximum seat height, measured from the floor to a clearly identified point on the seat. The difference is the hydraulic travel. Check whether that range allows each stylist to work without excessive bending and whether the chair stays at the selected height under its declared load.

A credible specification should state more than hydraulic chair. For comparison, the official Takara Belmont Apollo 2 specification identifies seat height, overall dimensions, equipment weight, a hydraulic base and lockable rotation. This is an example of useful manufacturer documentation, not an endorsement and not evidence that an unbranded chair has the same performance.

Rotation lock, base and stability

Verify whether rotation can be locked, how the lock is engaged and whether it holds when the client enters, exits or reclines. Request base diameter or base-plate dimensions, assembled weight and a video showing the chair raised, rotated, locked and loaded. A wide base can still be unstable if the pump mounting, frame or fasteners are weak.

For reclining models, request evidence with the back upright and fully reclined. Check for rocking, sudden movement, floor contact by the footrest and excessive flex around the pump. Do not use seller phrases such as heavy-duty as a substitute for measurable evidence.

Load rating and client fit

Request the manufacturer-declared human load rating and the document or label carrying it. One official Takara Belmont hydraulic chair manual, for example, specifies a 135-kilogram-force maximum human-body load for the covered product. That number applies to that documented product only. It must not be transferred to another chair because the designs look similar.

Also measure usable seat width between the armrests, seat depth, back height and footrest position. A high load claim does not prove that the seat comfortably accommodates the intended client population.

Recline, headrest and footrest

Confirm whether recline is manual, hydraulic or electrically powered; whether it locks at intermediate positions; and whether the headrest is removable and height-adjustable. Determine whether the footrest is fixed, independently moving or synchronised with the backrest. Ask for the fully upright and fully reclined dimensions because the recline envelope affects salon layout.

Upholstery and cleaning compatibility

Ask for the exact upholstery material, colour code and cleaning instructions. PU leather, PVC vinyl and genuine leather should not be treated as interchangeable. Hair dye, disinfectants, alcohol, bleach, acetone and aggressive cleaners may stain or damage some surfaces.

An official manufacturer care manual for a barber chair recommends a soft cloth and suitably diluted neutral detergent for its covered upholstery while warning against solvents, abrasives and bleach. Follow the cleaning instructions for the exact chair purchased and test any approved product on an inconspicuous area. Do not accept a generic claim that every chemical is safe.

Spare pumps, warranty and return logistics

A hydraulic chair can become unusable because of a pump, seal, pedal, bearing, recline cable or mounting component. Before ordering, ask whether the pump is a standard replaceable size, whether a complete replacement pump can be purchased separately and whether seals or internal repairs are supported locally. Obtain the spare-part code, price basis and expected availability in writing.

Warranty duration alone is not enough. The official Takara Belmont warranty policy illustrates how coverage may differ between hydraulic cylinders, upholstery, mechanical components and other parts, with installation and maintenance conditions affecting eligibility. Your supplier may offer completely different terms.

  • When does the warranty begin: payment, dispatch, arrival or installation?
  • Does it cover parts, labour or both?
  • Who diagnoses a fault and what video or serial-number evidence is required?
  • Who pays transport if a chair or pump must be returned?
  • Can the supplier send a replacement part instead of requesting the entire chair?
  • Is there a technician or parts contact in Nigeria?

Do not dismantle hydraulic, reclining or electrical equipment merely to preserve a claim. Follow the written procedure and use qualified technicians.

Measure the salon, access route and work area

Start with a scaled floor plan. Record the assembled width and depth of each chair, its base footprint, maximum reclined depth and space used by the stylist, trolley and client. Mark the proposed equipment positions on the floor before ordering. Avoid universal spacing claims: the right clearance depends on chair movement, services, building access and professional layout requirements.

Measure every doorway, corridor, stair landing and lift between the delivery vehicle and final position. Compare those measurements with both packed-carton and assembled dimensions. Confirm whether arms, backrests, bases or basins can be removed safely for access and who is responsible for reassembly.

For wash stations, map the water supply, drain position and service access. For wall-mounted mirrors or stations, identify wall construction and obtain professional advice on fixings. Electrical equipment and wash-unit installation

Powered dryers, processors, sterilising devices, illuminated mirrors and electric chairs require a clear nameplate photo showing rated voltage, frequency, wattage or current, model number and plug. A supplier statement that an item is suitable for Nigeria is not sufficient. A qualified electrician should compare the plug and voltage specification with the actual premises supply, outlets, protection and generator or inverter arrangement.

Wash units require professional plumbing assessment and fitting. Ask for an installation diagram, inlet and outlet sizes, recommended water pressure, hot-water requirements and a complete list of supplied fittings. A product page saying easy plumbing integration does not prove that the mixer, spray head, flexible hoses, trap or locally compatible connectors are included. Test water flow and drainage professionally before the unit enters full service.

For an exact-product standards or importability question, use the Standards Organisation of Nigeria enquiry channel and the Nigeria Customs Service official website as starting points. Do not assume that a manual chair, electrical dryer and UV device receive the same assessment.

Other salon and barbershop equipment to specify

  • Mirrors and workstations: dimensions, edge finish, mounting method, electrical contents and packing protection.
  • Trolleys: tray material, caster quality, wheel locks, load per shelf and resistance to salon chemicals.
  • Stools: rated load, seat-height range, caster type and replaceable gas lift.
  • Dryers and processors: voltage, frequency, power, temperature controls, timers and replacement heating or control parts.
  • Clippers and handheld tools: model authenticity, blade availability, charger rating, battery details and warranty route.
  • Towel warmers or sterilising equipment: intended process, capacity, timer, temperature or lamp specification and exact-product assessment.

Keep furniture procurement separate from skincare packaging decisions. Bottles, jars, labels and formulas involve a different specification and sourcing process.

Test supplier claims before a bulk order

A salon furniture supplier may be a factory, trading company, distributor or retailer. Attribute every capacity statement to the party making it. If a supplier claims a particular monthly output, ask which factory produces the chair, which processes are in-house, what quantity is already committed and how the proposed order will be inspected and packed.

Use the China supplier verification checklist to compare company identity, payment beneficiary, product history and evidence. Where ownership or production capacity remains unclear, a supplier intelligence enquiry can be considered before a material order.

A sample should match the intended production specification. Record upholstery colour, foam firmness, stitching, frame finish, pump model, base, armrests, footrest, carton and labels. During inspection, test repeated lifting, controlled lowering, rotation lock, recline, headrest adjustment, fasteners, finish and packaging. The principles in a documented factory test run are also useful here: define acceptance evidence before production rather than requesting a vague final video.

Use packed CBM, not visual size

Salon chairs are bulky relative to their value. Ask for the packed length, width and height of every carton, the number of cartons per unit, gross weight and whether the chair is shipped assembled or knocked down.

Consider a purely hypothetical model: four chair cartons each measuring 0.86 m by 0.70 m by 0.72 m would occupy 0.43344 CBM each, or 1.73376 CBM in total. A wash-unit carton measuring 1.25 m by 0.75 m by 1.00 m would add 0.9375 CBM. The modelled shipment would therefore occupy 2.67126 CBM before any pallet or protective overpack. These are teaching figures, not supplier packing data or a freight quotation.

Local single purchase versus bulk import

Decision factor Local single purchase Bulk import
Inspection Buyer may inspect the actual unit before acceptance Requires sample approval, inspection evidence or defined acceptance criteria
Cash commitment Usually limited to one or a few immediately needed units Product, packaging, freight and other order costs are committed across a larger quantity
Specification control Limited to locally available models and colours May support coordinated colours, branding or specifications if the quantity justifies production
After-sales logistics Potentially simpler if the seller keeps parts and accepts local returns Replacement parts and warranty transport must be planned before shipment
Unit economics No international freight allocation, but the seller has already absorbed import and inventory costs Can improve at sufficient scale, but only after all landed and failure costs are allocated

For one beauty salon chair needed immediately, local buying may be the more rational decision even when the sticker price appears higher. It allows physical inspection and avoids spreading international procurement work across one unit. Bulk importing becomes more attractive when several stations or branches need standardised equipment and the landed cost remains sensible after freight, clearing assumptions, inspection, local delivery, installation, spares and defects.

Quote, sample and inspection checklist

  1. Define whether each item is a styling chair, barber chair, wash unit, stool, trolley, mirror or powered device.
  2. State quantity, colour, upholstery code, base finish and permitted variation.
  3. Request assembled dimensions, reclined dimensions, packed dimensions, carton count and gross weight.
  4. Request minimum and maximum seat height, hydraulic travel, lock method and rated human load.
  5. Identify pump brand or model and obtain a quote for at least one compatible spare pump where appropriate.
  6. Confirm headrest, footrest, recline mechanism and every loose accessory included in the price.
  7. For wash units, itemise the basin, mixer, spray hose, drain fittings, inlets and installation diagram.
  8. For powered items, obtain the nameplate, plug and voltage details before approval.
  9. Obtain approved upholstery cleaning instructions and chemical limitations.
  10. Write the warranty period by component and the return, labour and replacement-part procedure.
  11. Approve a sample or detailed pre-production evidence before a large customised order.
  12. Issue an inspection checklist covering function, stability, finish, measurements, accessories, labels and packaging.
  13. Verify the payment beneficiary and supplier identity before sending funds.
  14. Calculate landed cost using confirmed packing data rather than catalogue dimensions.
  15. Arrange qualified electrical, plumbing and installation work where required.

If an exact product link and variant are already settled, buying from Chinese websites may suit a straightforward purchase. If the goods have already been purchased and only logistics remain, review shipping options. Neither route removes the need for a complete specification.

Source salon equipment as a business project

For a multi-chair setup, branch rollout or coordinated equipment order, prepare one schedule containing quantities, approved variants, dimensions, technical evidence, packing requirements, spare parts and acceptance checks. You can then submit a bulk salon equipment sourcing enquiry with enough detail for the requirement to be assessed without guessing. Include the delivery location and whether you need supplier search or already have selected product links.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a beauty salon chair cost in Nigeria?

There is no responsible single market figure without defining the chair. The verified examples checked on 9 October 2026 were ₦250,000 for one listed styling chair and ₦320,000 for one listed reclining hydraulic chair. These are seller-specific examples, not a market range, and delivery or installation was not confirmed as included.

Is a hydraulic styling chair suitable for barbering?

It may support basic haircutting, but shaving and reclined services can require a stable reclining backrest, adjustable headrest and suitable footrest. Confirm the service position, load rating and locked stability rather than relying on the product name.

Should I buy salon chairs locally or import them?

Local purchasing is often practical for one urgent chair or when physical inspection and local returns matter. Importing may make economic sense for a larger standardised order, provided the sample, supplier, packed CBM, landed cost, spare parts and warranty logistics are acceptable.

What is the most important salon chair specification?

No single figure is sufficient. Start with rated load, usable seat width, hydraulic height range, lock stability, base dimensions, service position and replacement-pump availability. A chair must fit both the client and the work area.

Does a wash-unit price normally include installation?

Do not assume it does. Obtain a written list of included fittings and a separate statement for delivery, plumbing, testing and installation. Use qualified professionals for water, drainage and electrical connections.
